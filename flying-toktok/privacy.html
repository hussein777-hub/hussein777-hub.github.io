Privacy Policy - Flying Tuk-Tuk

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how the game "Flying Tuk-Tuk" handles your information. The game is developed by Hussein.

Information we collect

The game does not require an account and does not collect your real name, phone number, or location.

Leaderboard: If you choose to save your score, the nickname you enter and your score are stored to display them on the leaderboard. Do not enter your real name or any personal information.

Settings: Your best score and chosen language are stored on your device only.

Advertising

The game may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and general technical information (such as device type, operating system, and approximate IP address) to serve ads and measure their performance. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
